3D Reconstruction Workflow

Transforming fragmented 2D land records into uniquely identifiable vertical property units.

1. Data Collection

  • Approved Floor Plans
  • Government Property Records
  • Surveyor Data (Measurements, Photos, LiDAR)

2. 3D Reconstruction

System processes multiple photos + measurements + floor plans to create accurate 3D models.

3. Unique Digital Identity

Flat gets a unique digital property identity connected to its parent land parcel.

4. Grampanchayat Verified

Final digital identity is officially verified and approved by the Grampanchayat.
